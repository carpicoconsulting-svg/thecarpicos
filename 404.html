Q&A

Questions and answers

For our friends and family who have questions, please review the answers below.

What is the RSVP deadline?

Please RSVP by July 15, 2027 so that we can get an accurate headcount. You can reply right here on the site.

Can I bring a plus one?

Plus ones are by invitation only. If your invitation includes a guest, there will be room to add their name when you RSVP.

Are children invited?

While we love your little ones, we have decided to keep our wedding a primarily adults only event.

Where can I park?

There is plenty of free parking at the front and back of the venue.

What should I wear?

The dress code is formal attire.

Is the wedding indoors or outdoors?

The ceremony will be outdoors, and the reception will be indoors. There are plenty of grassy and dirt areas during the ceremony, so opt for block heels or sandals instead of stilettos.

Is it okay to take pictures during the wedding?

Yes! Feel free to snap away with your phones and cameras, but remember there will be a photographer on site.

Who should I contact if I have questions?

Call or text us at 416-826-9078, or send us a note in the message box when you RSVP and we will get back to you.
